Rep

Android app · FastAPI backend · on Google Play

Google Play Website

Pick a target - 100 pull-ups for time, most push-ups in two minutes - log every rep against a live timer, and see who is still standing at the end of the month. Rep turns training into something you compete at, with friends or against the whole leaderboard.

  • Offline where it counts. Open the app in a basement gym with no signal, track a session and log it - the write queues in Room and drains through WorkManager when you reconnect.
  • Five ways to define a challenge. Rep goals, time limits, unbroken sets, max rounds, or free logging - one schema, one tracking screen.
  • Built to survive its own updates. New challenge types are withheld from older clients so one unknown enum can't blank a whole list.
  • Everything around the app too. Postgres and Alembic migrations, JWT and Google sign-in, Firebase push, Crashlytics, an admin dashboard, and the marketing site.

N

NexiTab

Chrome extension · Chrome Web Store

Chrome Web Store

The Chrome new tab, rebuilt as a page you arrange yourself: shortcut blocks for the sites you actually open, widgets you drag and resize onto a grid, and everything synced across the machines you sign in on.

  • A grid, not a template. Blocks and widgets are positioned and resized freely, and the layout travels with your account.
  • Keyboard first. Ctrl+K opens a command palette for every action, and each command can be bound to your own shortcut.
  • Timers that keep running. Minimise a timer, Pomodoro or countdown to the tray and it finishes in the background, then tells you.
  • Plugged into what you use. History, bookmarks and top sites in a side panel, Notion blocks as live to-do lists, plus import and export of your whole setup.
