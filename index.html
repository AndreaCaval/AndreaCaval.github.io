NexiTab

Chrome extension · Chrome Web Store

Chrome Web Store

A replacement for the Chrome new tab page. You group the sites you use into blocks, add widgets like timers, notes and weather, and arrange both on a grid that syncs across your devices.

  • Blocks and widgets are dragged and resized freely; layouts export and import.
  • Timers, Pomodoro and countdowns keep running in the background.
  • Ctrl+K command palette, plus history, bookmarks and Notion to-do lists in a side panel.

About

A bit about me

I'm a software engineer in Dublin. At Rue Gilt Groupe I work on backend services and AI-powered features for the group's flash-sale platforms, mainly in Python, Django and FastAPI, along with the React in front of them.

Before moving to Ireland I worked on 2D/3D spare-parts catalogue software in Italy, and from 2021 to 2022 I ran CavaScripts, a paid Chrome extension of my own. That one taught me the parts of a product that come after the code: releases, payments and support.

I build side projects because I want to use them myself, which is where Rep and NexiTab came from. Outside work I train, 3D print, and spend time on home automation and hardware.
